Why this kitchen exists

I started Silver Spoon Kitchen after spending a year cooking for my mother, who had cooked for forty years and suddenly found that the recipes she knew by heart no longer fit her hands, her appetite, or her kitchen. The recipes she found online were written for families of four, buried under three paragraphs of backstory, and printed in a typeface she had to squint at.

So this site is different by design. Every recipe is cut down to one or two servings. Steps are short and numbered. Measurements are given in cups and ounces, not just grams. And every recipe lists the protein, fiber, sodium, and calories per serving, because those numbers matter more after 60 than they did at 30.

Nothing here is published until it has been cooked at least twice in a home kitchen and eaten by someone who will say honestly if it is any good.
